Source. Anchor themes, questions, weights and scoring logic are taken from SeMS_PreCert_Predictive_Anchors_v1_30OCT25 (June 2025 version). Level definitions and evidence expectations are from that workbook's Summary sheet. Band thresholds match the IATA SeMS Certification Programme Checklist v3.0 (14 November 2025): 50–69% suitable, 70–89% operating, 90%+ effective, with every core component required to reach 50%.

Scoring. Each anchor is scored 1 / 3 / 4 / 5 and normalised to 20% / 60% / 80% / 100%. The overall figure is the mean across answered anchors. A predicted result is withheld until all 15 are scored. Any core component averaging below 50% forces a needs-development prediction regardless of the overall figure.

Status. Illustrative screening instrument. An anchor result is a triage signal for directing assurance effort — it does not confer, predict with certainty, or substitute for a certification decision.
